# Void Survivors — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Void Survivors** (the "App") handles information. The App is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us").

## Information we collect

**We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information** such as your name, email address, phone number or location.

- **Game progress** (coins, upgrades, unlocked ships, settings, high scores, the pilot name you choose) is saved **only on your device**. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App deletes it.
- **No account or sign-in** is required to play.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect information such as:

- your device's advertising ID,
- IP address and approximate location (country),
- device type, operating system and app interactions with ads,
- diagnostic and crash information related to ads.

This information is collected and processed by Google according to its own policies:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (**Settings → Google → Ads** on Android).

Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose whether to watch them (for example to revive or double your coins).

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data security and retention

Since we do not collect personal data, we do not store it. Information collected by Google AdMob is retained by Google under its policies.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
